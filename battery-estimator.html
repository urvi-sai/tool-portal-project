Battery Life Estimator

Many sensors sleep most of the time and wake up now and then to take a reading and send it. Enter how your device behaves and this tool estimates how long a battery will last. The numbers below are an example you can change.

Printed on the battery. mAh (milliamp-hours) is how much charge it holds.

How much electricity the device uses while it is working. mA means milliamps.

How long it stays awake to take and send one reading.

Sleeping uses very little. µA means microamps; 1000 µA is 1 mA.

How often the device wakes up, counted from one wake-up to the next.
